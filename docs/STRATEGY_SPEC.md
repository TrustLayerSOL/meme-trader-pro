# Strategy Specification

## Primary strategy: selective post-launch survival momentum

A candidate is considered only when post-launch momentum appears durable and wallet behavior indicates quality.

## Entry filters

- minimum liquidity
- minimum unique buyers
- positive buy/sell imbalance
- organic attention proxy
- no obvious rug-risk flags
- creator/dev not dumping
- acceptable quote impact/slippage

## Exit logic

- time stop
- trailing stop
- volume-decay exit
- dev/creator sell kill switch
- liquidity collapse kill switch

## Validation-first principle

No live execution until historical backtest, walk-forward validation, and paper trading agree.
